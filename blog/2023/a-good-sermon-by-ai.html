A Good Sermon - By AI

By Tinku Thompson · September 26, 2023

I recently asked ChatGPT a question - How should a good sermon look like? This response was very impressive, in my view. 

A good sermon is a form of religious or spiritual discourse typically delivered during a religious service, and it should aim to inspire, educate, and engage the congregation. While the specific content and style of a sermon can vary greatly depending on the religious tradition, denomination, and the individual preacher, there are some key elements that can contribute to the effectiveness of a sermon. Here are some guidelines for what a good sermon should look like:

  1. Clear Purpose and Message:

    • A good sermon should have a clear and well-defined purpose or message. The preacher should have a specific theme, topic, or scripture passage that they want to communicate to the congregation.

  2. Scriptural Foundation:

    • Many sermons are based on religious texts, such as the Bible or Quran. It's important for the preacher to provide a solid scriptural foundation for the message, including appropriate context and interpretation.

  3. Relevance:

    • A good sermon should be relevant to the lives of the congregation. It should address issues and concerns that are meaningful to the people in attendance and offer guidance and insights on how to apply religious teachings to their daily lives.

  4. Engaging Delivery:

    • The preacher should deliver the sermon in a way that is engaging and holds the congregation's attention. This can include using anecdotes, examples, humor (if appropriate), and effective storytelling techniques.

  5. Structure:

    • A well-structured sermon typically includes an introduction, body, and conclusion. The introduction should grab the congregation's attention and introduce the main topic. The body should provide the main content and supporting points, and the conclusion should summarize the message and offer a call to action or reflection.

  6. Clarity:

    • The preacher should speak clearly and concisely, avoiding jargon or overly complex language. The congregation should be able to understand and follow the message easily.

  7. Authenticity:

    • A good sermon is delivered with sincerity and authenticity. The preacher should speak from the heart and demonstrate a genuine connection to the message being shared.

  8. Application:

    • It's important to provide practical application of the message. The congregation should leave the sermon with a clear sense of how they can apply the teachings to their lives.

  9. Respect and Inclusivity:

    • A good sermon should be respectful of the diversity of the congregation. It should avoid language or topics that could be alienating or offensive to different groups within the community.

  10. Time Considerations:

    • The length of a sermon can vary widely, but it's important to be mindful of the congregation's attention span. Most sermons fall within a 20-30 minute range, but shorter or longer sermons can be effective if they are well-paced and engaging.


Ultimately, a good sermon should leave the congregation spiritually enriched, inspired, and motivated to live in accordance with their faith and values. It should foster a sense of community and provide a meaningful experience for those in attendance.
